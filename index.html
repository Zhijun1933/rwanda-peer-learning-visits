Analytical framework

Codes applied to the full texts of reports. Only counts are published; coded passages stay with the researcher. Full definitions: analytic codebook.

Research question: how does Rwanda use study visits to project an image of sovereign policy autonomy and to establish its status as a development leader among African states? Autonomy is measured against donors, status against African peers; each code belongs to one of these dimensions, or to the evidence that cuts against them.

About the data

Each record is one delegation's visit, compiled from published news reports, government releases and programme documents, with every source linked. Article texts are not redistributed.

Definitions and coding rules: codebook. Data and code: GitHub repository.

Reading it carefully

Coverage reflects what is reported. The Ministry of Defence publishes study tours systematically, so defence-college visits are likely over-represented relative to civilian ones. "Not mentioned" for Rwanda Cooperation means no source names it, not that it was uninvolved. Records marked snippet only or machine-read await a check by a person.
